      Frequently asked questions

      Is this a real loan application?

      No. Not Real Bank is fictional and nothing here is processed or stored.

      How does the model work?

      It is a hand-tuned logistic regression: it adds up weighted signals (debt-to-income, income, credit utilization, missed payments), then a sigmoid function converts the total into a probability between 0% and 100%. The weights here are made up for the demo, not trained on real data.

      What do the 10% and 30% thresholds mean?

      They are example bank rules. Below 10% probability of default the application is approved, from 10% to 30% it goes to human review, and above 30% it is rejected with reasons. Different banks set different rules.

      How is the monthly payment estimated?

      With a fixed 8% APR over the tenure you choose. Debt-to-income includes this new payment.
